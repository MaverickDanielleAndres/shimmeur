# SHIMMEUR ONE-PAGE LANDING PAGE — CONTENT & SECTION SPEC

## Page goal

Explain what Shimmeur does, establish Natalie Phillips' credibility, show genuine property/project evidence, and make it easy for the right property owner or referral partner to start a conversation.

This is one landing page, not a multi-page site.

Required:

- contact CTA

- Who We Help
- Our Work
- About
- Get in Touch

- Blog

- Login
- Shop
- unrelated service pages

unless a local client source explicitly requires them.

## 2. Hero

Source inspiration:
- website prototype
- brand guidelines
- creative property-manager flyer

Shimmeur helps unlock a property's potential/value through design-led renovation and end-to-end project management.

Preferred content direction:

Purpose:

Source-backed themes:

- life transitions make preparation hard
- owners may have limited time, resources, or expertise
- selling without thoughtful preparation can leave potential untapped

Tone:
empathetic, not alarmist.

Do not claim a property will definitely lose a specific dollar amount.

## 5. How It Works

Use the local proposal/prototype model.

Base sequence:

### 01 — Agree / establish current value
Agree on the property's current market value before work begins.

### 02 — Design and fund
Shimmeur creates the vision and, where the partnership model applies, funds the renovation.

### 03 — Manage and deliver
Shimmeur coordinates the trusted team and project delivery.

### 04 — Sell / settlement / agreed uplift share
Where the funded partnership model applies, costs are recovered at settlement and uplift is shared according to the agreement.

Important:
The process wording must not imply every Shimmeur service is always a funded JV unless the source documents establish that.

If the landing page offers both project-management services and a funded partnership model, make the distinction clear.

Any numerical example must be labelled illustrative and non-guaranteed.

## 6. Who We Help

### Deceased estates

### Long-term rental owners / rental refresh
Position around updating a property after tenancy or before sale.
