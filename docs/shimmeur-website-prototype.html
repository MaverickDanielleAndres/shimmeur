The reality

Most properties sell for less
than they should.

The stress of preparing a home for sale leads most owners to take shortcuts — or none at all. They sell as-is, buyers discount heavily for perceived work, and the gap between what a property could achieve and what it does is rarely recovered.

01

Overwhelm at the worst time

Preparing a home for sale during a life transition — estate, separation, downsizing — is exhausting. Most owners don't have the bandwidth to do it well.

02

Guesswork that's expensive

Without design expertise and renovation experience, most improvements either miss the mark or don't generate a meaningful return at auction.

03

Money left on the table

The gap between a property sold as-is and one properly prepared can be significant — sometimes hundreds of thousands. That gap is the Shimmeur opportunity.

The process

Signature Spaces.
Lasting Impressions.

Shimmeur provides a complete, end-to-end renovation management service. We design the vision, fund the project, manage the team, and deliver the transformation — so you can focus on what comes next.

1
We agree on your property's value

Together we establish the current market value of your property — the honest, agreed baseline everything else is built on.

2
We design the vision and fund the renovation

Shimmeur funds the renovation entirely. We design the transformation, source the trades, and carry the cost — no outlay required from you.

3
We manage everything — you stay hands-free

From first meeting to final styling, we handle every detail. Your role is to approve the vision and watch the transformation unfold.

4
At settlement, you receive your agreed share

Once the property sells, Shimmeur recovers the renovation cost from the proceeds. The uplift is split per our agreement — you receive your share, clean and clear.

An illustrative example
Agreed current value $1,000,000
Renovation investment (Shimmeur-funded) $100,000
Sale price achieved $1,400,000
Shimmeur cost recovery $100,000
Uplift to share $300,000
Your share (illustrative 50/50 split) $150,000

Split ratios vary by deal. This example is illustrative only and does not represent a guarantee of return. Actual outcomes depend on property, market, and agreed terms.
