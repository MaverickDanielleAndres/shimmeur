Wordmark Only — S Mark + Name

Use when the tagline would be illegible at small sizes, or in contexts requiring a compact but complete identity (app icons excluded).

The SHIMMEUR wordmark is always set in DM Sans Light (300 weight), letter-spacing 0.35em, uppercase. Never bold. The fineness of the letterforms contrasts the organic weight of the S mark.

Clear Space

Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the "S" letterform on all sides. Nothing — text, images, rules, or other logos — should encroach within this zone.

Dashed line = minimum exclusion zone

Minimum Size

Never reproduce the full logo smaller than the sizes below. At smaller dimensions, use the S mark monogram.

60px / 20mm — Digital minimum
45px / 15mm — Absolute minimum (wordmark only)
28px — Use monogram below this size

Logo Don'ts

✕ Don't recolour

Never apply unapproved colours to the logo. Use only the approved palette combinations shown in the logo suite.

✕ Don't rotate

The logo always sits upright and level. Never tilt, skew, or apply any rotation to any element.

✕ Don't stretch

Always scale proportionally. Never distort the logo by stretching or compressing in any direction.

✕ Don't place on busy backgrounds

Avoid placing the logo on photography or patterned backgrounds without a clean container or overlay.

✕ Don't add effects

No drop shadows, glows, bevels, emboss, or outlines. The logo stands on its own without enhancement.

Shimmeur
✕ Don't recreate in type

Never typeset the wordmark manually. Always use the approved logo file — never attempt to recreate it.

Colour Usage Principles

Navy leads

Navy is the dominant colour. It should occupy the majority of any dark composition — hero sections, headers, footers. It establishes authority and sophistication.

Sage accents

Sage and Light Sage are accent colours — they provide warmth and life to compositions without competing with the primary navy. Used on type accents, dividers, and eyebrows.

Cream breathes

Cream and white provide breathing room. Use as section backgrounds and card fills — never use pure white as the only background; cream adds warmth that pure white lacks.

Brand Voice & Tone

Every word is a brand impression. Shimmeur's voice is warm but precise — the authority of a seasoned property professional delivered with the care of someone who genuinely loves what they do.

Sophisticated, not cold

We use precise, intelligent language — but always with warmth. We never use jargon to impress. We explain the model clearly, confidently, and without condescension.

"We fund the renovation, manage every detail, and share the upside with you at settlement."
"We leverage our renovation capability to maximise vendor yield outcomes."

Confident, not arrogant

We know what we're doing. We've done this before. But we never boast — we demonstrate through clarity, results, and the quality of the process itself.

"We've managed renovations that have returned six figures more than expected."
"Nobody does it better than us."

Human, not transactional

We understand that the people we work with are often navigating something difficult — a loss, a transition, a long chapter closing. We meet them with care.

"We understand this is more than a property sale — and we're here to make it easier."
"We process deceased estate transactions efficiently."

Precise, not verbose

Nat values ease and simplicity. Every sentence should earn its place. We cut the filler. We say what we mean. We trust the reader to follow.

"No upfront cost. No guesswork. Just results."
"Our innovative and unique value proposition ensures that you as the client are not required to outlay any funds prior to the completion of the settlement process."

The Shimmeur voice in one sentence

"We are the person who cares deeply, delivers beautifully, and makes a complex thing feel effortless."

07
Photography & Imagery Direction

Shimmeur's visual world is warm, editorial, and aspirational — a lifestyle magazine aesthetic applied to property. Think Vogue Living, not real estate.com.au.

Interiors

Natural light. Wide-angle but not distorted. Styled with texture — linen, organic ceramics, fresh botanicals. Warm tones. Never overexposed or clinical. Always shot after staging.

Before & After

Consistent angle, consistent lighting (use flash for before; natural light for after). Never use wide-angle distortion to exaggerate the after. Let the work speak — it always does.

Portrait of Nat

On-location at a completed property where possible. Warm, confident, present — not posed. Natural colour grading; avoid filters that shift skin tones cool or grey. Dressed in neutral tones that complement the palette.

Colour grading

Warm highlights, slightly lifted shadows, muted but present greens. Think sage and linen — not cool, blue-toned real estate photography.

Stock imagery

Unsplash or Getty — filter for warm interiors. Avoid anything that looks like a real estate listing. Preferred photographers: Hutomo Abrianto, Jason Briscoe, Douglas Sheppard (Unsplash).

What to avoid

HDR composite skies. Oversaturated grass. Fisheye distortion. Dark moody lofts. Cold blue tones. Anything that could appear in a generic real estate database.
